Your overall direction is strong, but there is one change I'd make before you write any code.

Don't think of this as "a website that plays a song with 3D effects."

Think of it as an interactive real-time cinematic experience, where the website is only the delivery platform.

The two uploaded documents already provide an excellent foundation. They define a six-act narrative, a strong technical architecture, quality tiers, an audio-reactive engine, a timeline-driven lyric system, accessibility, and performance constraints. They are already much closer to an industry-grade design document than a typical PRD.  

What I would add is Version 2.0, focusing on the elements that create a true "wow" factor rather than simply adding more visual effects.

Upgrade the vision

Instead of:

> Song + Moon + Lyrics + Forest



Create:

> An emotional universe that reacts to the music.



Every visual element should feel alive.

Stars breathe.

Mountains subtly shift with the soundtrack.

Trees react to the wind and audio.

Mist curls around rocks.

Moonlight scatters through leaves.

Water reflects every particle.

Fireflies gather around lyric events.

Clouds slowly reveal and hide the moon.

Hidden Cinematic Moments

Avoid making every effect obvious.

Examples:

A shooting star appears only after the user stays still for 30 seconds.

A rare constellation forms only once per session.

Tiny fireflies spell a subtle symbol before dispersing.

At dawn, the last moon reflection fades before the moon itself disappears.


These restrained moments are often more memorable than constant visual intensity.

One recommendation I would not make

You mentioned "100 percent realistic moon, mountains, trees."

That isn't a realistic target for a browser running on typical hardware, especially mobile. Modern browsers can achieve highly realistic real-time graphics, but not true photorealism comparable to offline-rendered VFX. Aim for stylized cinematic realism—the level seen in high-end WebGL showcases—because it provides a much better balance of beauty, performance, and compatibility.

Overall, the PRD and agent.md you've uploaded are already an excellent architectural base. My additions are primarily about elevating the artistic direction and system design rather than changing the underlying architecture. They complement the existing documents instead of replacing them.
