---

One important design principle

The temptation is to include every visual effect at once. Resist that. The most memorable cinematic experiences use contrast: long stretches of calm, subtle motion interrupted by a few carefully timed "wow" moments. That restraint is what makes the highlights feel powerful rather than overwhelming.
